1. In addition to the usual comments to be incorporated into a reply to

Mr, Berridge by your office, it is suggested that the following paragraph be
included;

* "The details of the observation which you made were referred to our
Intelligence analysts for their study and comment. They have informed me
that, although the object could possibly have been a high altitude balloon
ascending through different wind strata, they find it impossible to arrive
at a definite conclusion. However, they were happy to receive the

information and to add it to the data from other sightings which they
process and anelyze constantly."

CHEE CDS, eal Boag

General Sanford's statements during his recent press conference, particularly those
dealing with current USAF attitude towards the subject and plans for the future.
Stressing his statement about “adequate but not frantic attention" should also hely.

3. Mr. Berridge's letter is returned herewith.

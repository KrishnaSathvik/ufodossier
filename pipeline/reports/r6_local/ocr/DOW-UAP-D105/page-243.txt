MEMORANDUM FOR; THE DIRECTOR OF NAVAL INTELLIGENCE

SUBJECT; (Unclassified) Unidentified Flying Objects Reporting

1. Attached for your information and action considered appro-
priate is a copy of Air Force Letter Mumber 200-5, dated 29 April
1952 which expresses the Air Force interest in unidentified flying
objects and provides for a reporting procedure.

2. To assist the Air Force in collecting information on this
subject, it would be appreciated if such data as may come to the
attention of Naval personnel on unidentified flying objects could
be reported to the Air Force rapidly and substantially as outlined
in paragraph 8c and Se of attached letter.

3. Contents of attachment may be used in any manner you desire
for the issuance of instructions on the reporting of unidentified
flying objects.

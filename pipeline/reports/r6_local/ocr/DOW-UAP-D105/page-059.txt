MEMORANDUM FOR RECORD
PROBLEM:

1. To prepare a non-military letter to Mr. Gerald W. Harrington
of Providence, Rhode Island.

FACTS AND DISCUSSION?

2. Ina letter to the USAF dated 9 Oct 52 Mr. Harrington
describes in detail an observation of an unidentified flying object
made aboard the SS Caronia on 5 Oct 52. ‘The data which he includes
is somewhat remarkable in that it is very unusual to receive such
details from the ordinary citizen. He even took the trouble to
write the ship's captain at a later date and obtain the ship's locae
tion at the time of the observation.

3. A reply to Mr. Harrington is attached.

4. Subject matter is not considered suitable for inclusion
in the Daily Staff Digest.

ACTION RECOMMENDED :
5. Because of Mr. Harrington's prominent professional position

and the excellence of his report, it is strongly recommended that the
attached reply be signed by General Samford.

6. Approval, signature and dispatch of attached reply.

MEMORANDUM FOR RECORD

PROBLEM
1. To prepare R& to OSI.
FACTS AND DISCUSSION

2. In a personal letter to General Samford, Mr. H. D. Springer
conveys information concerning the activities of one Samuel P. Jones
who has professed complete knowledge of the "flying saucers". According
to Mr. Springer, this Mr. Jones predicted the recent appearance of
unidentified objects over Washington, D.C. and vicinity some time before
it occurred. It is desired to have Mr. Springer contacted to determine
certain information bearing on Project Blue Book.

3. Another letter of anenymous origin intimates that there is some
unusual activity on a movie set in California. It is desired to refer
this letter to OSI for any action they consider warranted.

4. The R&R will accomplish the referrals mentioned above.

5. Subject matter will not be included in the Daily Staff Digest.
ACTION RECOMMENDED

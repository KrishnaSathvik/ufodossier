Capt Smith/AC/75990
19 December 1952

MEMORANDUM FOR RECORD

PROBLEM:

1. To return a photographic negative purported to be
of "flying saucers" taken by a member of the USCG at Salem
Air Station, Massachusetts, on 16 July 1952.

FACTS AND DISCUSSION:

2. On 15 December 1952, Mrs. Mead of the Public Informa-
tion Division of the USCG here in Washington telephoned Captain
Smith of AFOIN-2A2 and requested return of subject negative.

3. Captain Smith contacted Captain Ruppelt of ATIC on
16 December 1952 by telephone and requested he bring the negative
to HQ USAF on a staff visit which he was to make on 17-18 December.

4. Captain Ruppelt stated that ATIC was finished with

analyzing the photograph and that he would bring it with him
for return to the USCG.
ACTION:

5- Subject negative returned by attached letter in com-
pliance with the request of the USCG.

COORDINATION:

AFOIN-2A2, Col. W. H. Smith, Ext. 71016
AFOIN-2A, Col. W. A. Adams, Ext. 74072

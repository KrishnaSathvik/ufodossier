sure that the hoax will be exposed by telling you that this is nothing more than
the silver iodide used in artificial rainmaking. This is especially true if the
silver iodide came in contact with radioactive clouds or so called thunderclouds.

The solution of this hoax is quite simple, if you will check your records
you will find that flying saucers were first seen about the time when silver
i6dide came into usage for artificial rainwaking. Again, silver is a reflective
agency and will reflect all types of light, which accounts for the various hues
reported by sky gazsers. Again, silver iodide, if radioactive or ionized by an
electrical cloud will be come flnorescent, which accounts for flyers being unable
to overtake the so called flying saucers, and their habit of disappearing.

Of course, we must eliminate such reports of flying saucers as were accountable
by balloons, foo-fighters, meteorites, and the person who just calls up a newspaper
with the hope of getting his name in the papers. Also to be disqualified are such
reports as to ascertain that they ere interplanetary space ships. If it were at
all possible for another planet to send aircraft of any type to our atmosphere,
it would simply burn up the same as a meteorite and such space ship remains would
be found the same as meteorites. As far as I kmoyv, no such wreckage has ever been
found.

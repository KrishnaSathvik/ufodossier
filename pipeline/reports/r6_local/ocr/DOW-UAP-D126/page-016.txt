Solution in Between Two Extremes 
With chemical propulsion, manned space flight to the Moon is barely possible and only 
with massive multistage rockets. For manned space flight beyond the Moon, nuclear 
propulsion is indispensible. Nuclear thermal propulsion is really not much better than 
advanced chemical propulsion. Ion propulsion, using a nuclear reactor to drive an 
electric generator, has a much higher specific impulse but not enough thrust for short 
interplanetary transit times needed for manned missions. This leaves propulsion by a 
chain of fission bombs (or fission-triggered fusion bombs) as the only credible option.

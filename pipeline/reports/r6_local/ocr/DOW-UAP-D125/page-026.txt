The design of the ion source also depends on the ion species being injected. Sources 
described thus far have focused on deuterium or, in some cases on trit ium. However, 
workers at the University of Wisconsin (U. Wisc.) have had a great interest in 3He 
reactions (both D-3He and 3He-3 He), so have developed a gun specialized to 3He ion 
production (Reference 2.8). To maximize the ion current, a Helicon source was selected 
since Helicons are well known for production of very high density plasmas from which 
high ion currents can be extracted. This source is illustrated in Figure 2.6 and discussed 
in Reference 2.8. They state that this source has produced steady-state ion currents of 
10 mA into IEC systems with background gas pressures as low as 200 µtorr.

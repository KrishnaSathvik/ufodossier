Bulk Slab Solid-State Laser 
Under the auspices of the Joint High Power Solid-State Laser (JHPSSL) program, 
Northrop Grumman broke the all-important 100 kW beam power threshold in early 
2009 by firing a 7-amplifier chain bulk slab solid-state laser (BSSSL) that produced a 
beam output power of almost 106 kW (see Figure 19). Each amplifier chain is 
assembled with several high-gain power modules. The laser operated for more than 
five minutes, achieved electro-optical efficiency of 19.3%, and reached full power in 
less than 0.6 seconds with a beam quality of better than 3.0, Whereas the test 
demonstration saw five minutes of continuous operation for the laser, altogether the 
system has been operated at above 100 kW beam power for a total duration of more 
than 85 minutes. Adding an eight amplifier chain that the system was designed for will 
increase the beam power to 120 kW. 
Figure 19. Northrop Grumman's Joint High Power Bulk Slab Solid-State Laser (courtesy of 
the Directed Energy Professional Society).

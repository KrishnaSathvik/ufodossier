(Question from audience) 
• "Is it unlikely, or rather, is it likely that any of these magazines have facts or data which 
they would withhold just for the sake of being sensational?" 
• "Life magazine has a lot of information that we do not have on different sightings. They 
have some very good sightings too. I mean, they've called in some pretty important 
people around the United States. They won't, they're very hesitant to tell you who works 
for them as their consultants. But they have got some pretty good sightings that we have 
never heard of, and they're going to use those in their article, although they have checked 
those with us. The two or three old standbys that everybody always plays up, such as this 
Mantell case down in Kentucky where this guy got killed chasing one of them, 
supposedly, the one up in Sioux Falls, South Dakota where the F-51 pilot fought with this 
thing for 15 minutes, and a few of those that are outstanding as far as the science fiction 
writers are concerned, Life is going to leave out. They're not going to dig those up again 
and rehash them again."

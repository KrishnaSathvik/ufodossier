• "Is this still classified top secret, since Life is coming out with it?" 
• "No, uh, we've, we actually, we carry it as confidential, the overall project. We're trying 
to get it dropped down to restricted now because Life is going to come out with a lot of 
material we've given them that is classified, or was classified at one time. The only time 
this was classified top secret was right after it came out. We've been carrying this 
confidential. There are some aspects of it we'll have to keep classified as secret or maybe 
higher when we start listing Air Defense radar stations, possible tie-in with some of our 
latest developments and things like that. We'll have to keep that classified. But just, or 
just the details of a sighting, we're going to try to declassify to restricted because we've 
had trouble in the past on that. Some newspaper will call up and say, 'Big sighting up 
somewhere,' and we'll say, 'Yes, we've heard about it.' And they'll say, 'What's the results?' 
We'll say, 'We can't tell you about that, it's classified.' So immediately they come out with 
a big story about, 'The Air Force just picked up a spaceship somewhere and they won't 
tell us about it.' And that's the thing we're trying to get away from. I mean, we don't want 
to excite anybody on the project."

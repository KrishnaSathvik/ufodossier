4. This report will be made at expiration of leave by the immediate
superior of the officer to whom the leave is granted and forwarded
immediately to the Chief of Naval Personnel. Care must be taken to

the day of return as a day of leave, except when such return is

not detached from ship or station (see Article 1727, Navy Regulations).

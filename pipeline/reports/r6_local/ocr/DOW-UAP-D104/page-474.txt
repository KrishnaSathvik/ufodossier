From: Commanding Officer, USS ESSEX (CVA9)
Tos All Hands, USS ESSEX (CVA9)
Commander, air Task Group 201

Subj: Commendation for Operations performed during
Mediterranean and WesPac Deployment

1. The Commanding Officer is in receipt of many complimentary
communications-concerning the outstanding performance of ESSEX
and Air Task Group 201 during the period 2 February to

17 November 1958.

2e During this period ESSEX and her embarked Air Group served
as a striking unit of the Sixth Fleet in Mediterranean waters,
and her aircraft provided initial and sustained air support
for U. 5. Marine landings in Lebanon from 15 July to August
1958. These operations, under wartime conditions, were per-
formed by the men of ESSEX and ATG 201 with an outstanding
degree of professional skill and devotion to duty.

3. Following this, ESSEX was deployed on an emergency basis
to the Far East, where air operations in support of U. 5S.
National policy during the Formosa crisis were conducted in
the same outstanding manner.

4. Indicative of the performance of ESSEX and-her Air Group
are the following excerpts from various communications
received:

a. Ina letter of March 28, 1958, RADM N. S. HENDERSON,
O.B.E., RN, Vice Naval Deputy, Supreme Headquarters, Allied
Powers Europe who was on board observing operations, stated....
"But above all I took away an impression of a quietly effi-
cient ship with a very lively crew, all of whom seemed keen
and interested in the job. I was deeply impressed...."

far the easiest and most well known way to generate (static) negative energy in the 
lab. The Casimir effect that is famil iar to most people is the force that is associated 
with the quantum vacuum electromagnetic ZPF [47]. This is an attract ive force that 
must exist between any two neutral (uncharged), parallel, flat, conducting surfaces 
(e.g ., meta llic plates) in a vacuum. This force has been well measured and it can be 
attributed to a minute imbalance in the vacuum electromagnetic ZPE density inside the 
cavity between the conducting surfaces versus the vacuum electromagnetic ZPE density 
in the free -space region outside of the cavity [ 48-50]. See Figure 2 for a schematic of 
the Casimir effect.

The act of squeezing transforms the phase space circular noise profile characteristic of 
the vacuum into an ellipse, whose semimajor and semiminor axes are given by unequal 
quadrature uncertainties (of the quantized electromagnetic oscillator operators). This 
applies to coherent states in general, and the usual vacuum is also a coherent state 
with eigenvalue zero. As this ellipse rotates about the origin with angular frequency w, 
these unequal quadrature uncertainties manifest themselves in the electromagnetic 
field oscillator energy by periodic occurrences, which are separated by one quarter 
cycle, of both smaller and larger fluctuations compared to the unsqueezed vacuum .

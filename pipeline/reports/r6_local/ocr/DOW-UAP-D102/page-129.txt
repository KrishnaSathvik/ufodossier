1. Reference (a) directed the interpretation of movies

of unidentified objects taken in Utah, 1952. Included
among the enclosures are approximately 30 feet of pro-
cessed 16mm color motion picture film and a list of ques-
tions for guidance in determining the type of analysis
desired. This progress report presents the procedures

and methods designed to answer some of the questions con-
tained in enclosure 7 and the results obtained as of this
date by the Photogrammetry Division, Evaluation Department
of this Center,

2. Tests and Procedure. The film exposed in Utah is in
very poor condition; dry, brittle and with a tendency to
break. After initial viewing, duplication of the film
was performed in order to preserve the one originally re-
ceived. This consumed approximately two weeks. In the
duplication process, some color correction was made to
permit better viewing of the objects. For convenience

in interpretation, the film was divided into three parts,

MEMORANDUM FOR RECORD
PROBLEM:
Tremont, Utah, sighting.
FACTS AND DISCUSSION:

On 15 Dec 52 Lt A. G. Flues telephoned Lt Malcom Ross, USN, at the Of-
fice of Naval Research, University of Minnesota. The purpose was to deter~
mine whether pillow balloon releases could account for the Tremont} Utah,
incident of 2 Jul 52.

Lt Ross had seen the Tremont/movies and it was his opinion, as well as
that of Lt Lewis, ONR, that the objects photographed were pillow balloons,
However, Lt Ross said that the Navy had not reléased any pillow balloons in
or near that area at that time. Lt Ross pointed out that General Mills, Inc.,
Minneapolis, is, in so far as he knows, the sole manufacturer of pillow bal-
loons and suggested that ATIC contact Mr. J. R. Smith of General Mills to
determine what organizations and ageneies had purchased pillow balloons. Lt
Ross also stated that in the winter of 1952, the "Crusade for Freedom" pur-=
chased many pillow balloons from General Mills. These were sent to various
cities in the United States and released periodically. Lt Ross did not know
the exact times, dates, or points of these releases, but he suggested that
ATIC contact the "Crusade for Freedom",

RECOMMENDATION:

That ATIAA-5 contact Mr. J. R. Smith and the "Crusade for Freedom",

The objects in the "Utah" and “Montana” films can only be correlated on
the basis of two rather weak points. First, their structure, or rather lack
of it, is similar. Tims as shown in the "blow-ups", figures 6a and 6b, there

of the frames show clusters of objects seemingly also grouped in pairs. See

Early Commercialization of Smart Materials 
In the third quarter of 2009, RavenBrick LLC introdu ced RavenWindow, an "active­
passive thermoreflective" window film that is transpare nt when cold and partially 
reflective when hot. The dual-purpose smart film is intended to manage and harness 
solar heat gain in windows. The film costs much less than do existing smart window 
technologies and offers superior energy savings.

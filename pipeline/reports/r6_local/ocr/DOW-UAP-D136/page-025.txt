This glucose sensor approach has been found to have limitations in stability when 
introduced into tissue or blood. Primarily the problems, as with many sensors, arise 
from the system chemistry and not as much from the electronic port ion of the sensor . 
The glucose oxidase enzyme slowly decays over time and thus the sensor sensitivity 
and calibration drifts making it eventually unusable.

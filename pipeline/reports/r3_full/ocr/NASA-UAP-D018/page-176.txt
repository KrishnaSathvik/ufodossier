SM (JRG:evd)

JUL 1 4 1965

TO: Manned Spacecraft Center
Chief, Photographic Division, Code bT2

FROM: NASA Headquarters
Chief, Inflight Sciences

SUBJECT: Request for duplicate movie films and color prints of
all 70 mm, hand-held pictures

One copy of each of color movie films which include sunsets,
sunrises, horizon bands, limb of earth and terrain views is re-
quested to be forwarded to Dr. J.R. Gill, Code SM, NASA Head-
quarters. Please send these air mail since this material is
needed soon for preparation of an astrongpical report on GT-4.
One of the most important films for this purpose is Magazine 9.

Two sets of color prints of all color still pictures which were
taken with Hasselblad 70 mm camera (on GT-4) are also requested.

It will be adequate to send these by regular mail, These prints
pertain mainly to the 8-5 and S-6 Gemini Experiments,

Jocelyn ‘R. Gill

Jocelyn R. Gill

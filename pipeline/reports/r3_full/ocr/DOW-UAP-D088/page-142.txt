On night of 27 July 48, at 2200 hours observer saw object which
resembled a Imge ball in the sky. It come from the south and

wes headed north traveling at speed of jet planes which often

fly over observer's house. Object trailed a bright red tail of fire
and traveled in a straight line. There wes some lightning and
thunder at the time, but ixam observer thought the object something
entirely foreign. No noise could be heard. Object only seen for
three seconds,

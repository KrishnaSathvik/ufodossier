CASE 2024-INDOPACOM-0412 // INFRARED

Football-shaped object near Japan

U.S. Indo-Pacific Command submitted a 2024 image capture of an apparent football-shaped body intercepted by an infrared sensor over the western Pacific. The object's velocity, altitude, and origin remain unresolved across the official intelligence assessment.

"...the object exhibited no observable thermal exhaust signature. Track lasted 0:47 before sensor lost contact at the eastern edge of the engagement window."
